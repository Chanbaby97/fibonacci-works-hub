What you'll learn

  • What your soil needs, and how to build it up naturally
  • How to choose seeds that do well in Cape Breton's climate
  • When to start seeds indoors, and when to plant outside
  • Spacing, watering and sunlight: the basics done right
  • How to watch for frost and use the local forecast

What to bring

Just yourself, no experience needed.

You'll take home soil, pots, a small shovel, gloves, seeds, seedlings, a seasonal planting calendar, a soil quick card and a Certificate of Completion.

Try at home

  1. Dampen the potting mix. Fill the tray loosely, don't pack it down hard.
  2. Plant seeds as deep as the packet says, or set a seedling no deeper than it grew.
  3. Label each tray with the plant and the date. Keep it in bright light and water until moist, not puddled.
  4. Toughen up seedlings over several days: a little more time outside each day, then back in.
  5. Put tender plants out only once the local forecast is past frost. Cool crops can go out earlier.
